Bring Your Community to AquaVaults

AquaVaults helps communities turn attention into participation. Whether you are building an NFT community, a token community, or something that blends both, a listing on AquaVaults gives your project a place to be discovered, supported, and actively chosen on-platform.

Share in the platform fees your activity helps generate
Potential Co-op Mining Rig rewards
Optional Discord-capable notifications
Apply for Listing

Applications are submitted directly to AquaVaults for review, with no wallet or application fee required. If a Connector recommended AquaVaults, you may optionally enter their code on the form.

Learn more about joining

Your community can gain visibility, share in the platform fees its activity helps generate, and unlock tools built to keep leaders informed and communities engaged. Fee share applies to platform fees that swaps actually generate and depends on confirmed swaps and current program rules — it is not guaranteed revenue. Listed communities also have opportunities to benefit from potential rewards tied to the Co-op Mining Rig, creating another layer of value beyond visibility alone.

What you get

  • A dedicated community listing on AquaVaults
  • The ability to share in the platform fees your activity helps generate
  • Potential rewards through the Co-op Mining Rig
  • Access to the AquaVaults Discord for community leaders
  • Optional Discord-capable notifications for important events
  • Visibility to users looking for communities to support
  • Access to future community-focused tools as AquaVaults expands

Who this is for

We are looking for NFT communities, token communities, and hybrid projects with real identity, active presence, and people who want to build. Newer projects can apply too, but we care a lot about quality, consistency, and whether the community is genuinely showing up.

How to join

Use the public community application to introduce your project. An application is not a listing approval, and you can leave optional fields blank. If you have a Connector code, enter it on the form and verify it before submitting; the introduction remains a candidate until AquaVaults independently verifies it.
