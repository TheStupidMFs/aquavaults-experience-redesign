Part B

AquaVaults Rewards Program Rules

1. Program overview

  • The AquaVaults Rewards Program recognizes verified participation across eligible Solana features and listed communities.
  • Reward cycles follow UTC calendar months, beginning at 00:00:00 UTC on the first day and ending immediately before 00:00:00 UTC on the first day of the following month.
  • Participants earn points under the point schedule published for that cycle. Points determine leaderboard position and proportional reward weight.
  • Points do not have a fixed dollar value and cannot be transferred, sold, purchased, withdrawn, or redeemed independently.
  • The reward pool is funded under AquaVaults treasury-allocation rules and may change between cycles. No minimum pool or reward amount is guaranteed unless separately published.

2. Eligibility and permanent wallet enrollment

  • Participation is available globally where permitted by applicable law and these rules.
  • A participant must control and enroll one Solana wallet by signing the published enrollment message.
  • The enrolled wallet is permanent for the rewards account. AquaVaults does not provide wallet recovery, replacement, migration, reset, or reward redirection.
  • A participant must be legally permitted to participate and must have authority to use the enrolled wallet.
  • AquaVaults may refuse, withhold, block, or cancel participation or distribution where required by law, sanctions restrictions, security concerns, or these rules.

3. Published cycle schedule

  • Before each cycle begins, AquaVaults publishes the point schedule, maximum available points, 30% eligibility threshold, frozen listed-community roster, and applicable rules version.
  • Scoring rules and eligibility requirements do not change retroactively during a cycle, except for a published system-wide waiver or correction needed to address an unavailable task or technical error.
  • Mid-cycle community listings begin counting in the next cycle.

5. Qualifying swaps

  • Each qualifying swap must be submitted by the enrolled wallet and finalize successfully.
  • Each qualifying swap must sell at least $5 USD-equivalent of canonical SOL, USDC, or USDT and collect a verified platform fee of at least 1%, up to the supported 10% maximum.
  • Manual Swap, AutoSwap, Flip-Flop, and approved AquaVaults SDK-widget execution may qualify equally.
  • Only unique, verified transactions count. Failed, duplicated, test, internal, manipulated, or unverifiable transactions do not count.
  • A community with a 100% community fee share remains fully eligible for community-support points.

6. Deferred from Rewards V1: Limit Orders

  • Limit-order activity does not earn points in AquaVaults Rewards V1 and does not count as a Product Explorer surface.
  • Limit Orders remain available as a separate AquaVaults product, but product availability does not imply Rewards eligibility.
  • Future inclusion requires a separate limit-order modernization and authoritative fill-verification project covering order lifecycle, fee routing, fill evidence, valuation, and wallet/order attribution.
  • Any future Limit Order reward rules must be published under a later rules and point-snapshot version before the affected cycle begins.
  • No pre-enrollment or pre-publication Limit Order activity is reserved for retroactive credit.

7. Loyalty Community and community roster

  • A participant selects one Loyalty Community for the cycle. AquaVaults may be selected.
  • The selection is locked for the cycle and cannot be changed voluntarily.
  • If the selected community becomes unavailable, the operator may authorize one replacement and preserve previously completed loyalty days.
  • The cycle’s listed-community roster is frozen at cycle start. AquaVaults is excluded from the every-community roster because it has separate tasks.
  • An unavailable listed community may be waived for every participant under a published operator decision.

8. Co-Op participation

  • The daily maximum requires one Mine contribution and two separate Hashboost contributions in different buckets.
  • One Droplet is sufficient per contribution.
  • Ordinary and Onchain Contribution Mode count equally.
  • Only successfully credited records count. If no eligible bucket is open, the participant must return later.

9. LP fee claiming and community routing

  • The LP quest requirement is at least $5 USD-equivalent in cumulative verified gross LP fees claimed during the cycle.
  • At least 10% of every counted claim must route to a valid, routing-enabled listed community.
  • Gross fees before the split are valued at claim confirmation. Multiple qualifying claims may combine.
  • AquaVaults does not satisfy the LP community-routing requirement.

10. Sequential monthly quest and task stacking

  • Ordinary tasks may stack. One verified action may earn its normal activity points and complete other applicable tasks.
  • The monthly quest remains strictly sequential. Later quest points cannot be awarded before earlier quest steps are completed.
  • Verified community progress is preserved while later community steps remain locked.
  • Quest Step 6 begins only after Step 5 is completed. It requires at least $25 in cumulative qualifying AquaVaults-listing swap value within one UTC day. Only swaps whose finalized transaction slot is strictly greater than the authoritative Step 5 completion slot count; earlier and same-slot swaps do not carry forward.
  • Verified LP activity may be recorded before Step 8 unlocks, but its quest points remain withheld until Step 8 is available.

  • A three-day (72-hour) reconciliation and review period follows cycle close. Finalization cannot occur before that period ends.
  • Verified on-chain activity is assigned according to its confirmed block time, not the time it was discovered by a poller.
  • Only finalized points are used for distribution.

12. Eligibility and reward calculation

  • A participant must earn at least 30% of the adjusted cycle maximum, rounded up to the next whole point.
  • An individual participant’s inability or decision not to complete an available task does not reduce that participant’s maximum-point denominator.
  • Eighty percent of the finalized reward pool is distributed proportionally among eligible wallets based on finalized points.
  • Twenty percent is used for leaderboard placement bonuses: 10% for first, 6% for second, and 4% for third.
  • Top-three wallets also receive their proportional share from the 80% pool.

13. Placement ties

  • Tied wallets share the placement slots occupied by the tie.
  • Two wallets tied for first divide the combined first- and second-place 16%; the next wallet receives the third-place 4%.
  • Three or more wallets tied for first divide the full 20% placement pool equally.
  • Wallets tied for second divide the combined second- and third-place 10%.
  • Wallets tied for third divide the third-place 4%.
  • No enrollment-time, wallet-address, or point-achievement-time tiebreaker is used.
  • USDC rounding is performed in base units. Any indivisible remainder returns to the proportional pool.

14. USDC distribution

  • AquaVaults manually sends finalized rewards in USDC to the enrolled wallet. Participants do not submit a reward claim.
  • Distribution timing may depend on reconciliation, review, treasury processing, network conditions, compliance requirements, and operational availability.
  • AquaVaults is not responsible for a participant’s loss of wallet access or inability to use the wallet after distribution.
  • A completed distribution is recorded with its cycle, wallet, amount, timestamp, and transaction signature.

15. Prohibited conduct

  • Submitting false, manipulated, duplicated, self-conflicting, or unverifiable activity.
  • Attempting to exploit point calculation, tracking delays, price sources, referral data, wallet sessions, or task state.
  • Using automation or coordinated wallets primarily to evade limits, multiply account allowances, or manufacture eligibility.
  • Interfering with AquaVaults, third-party protocols, other participants, or community routing.
  • Using the program in violation of applicable law or sanctions restrictions.

16. Operator authority and no guaranteed appeal

  • Only the operator or an authorized super admin may hold, restore, reverse, correct, disqualify, finalize, or distribute program records.
  • Operator and super-admin decisions are final. The program does not provide a guaranteed appeal or reconsideration process.
  • AquaVaults may correct verified technical, accounting, or administrative errors at its discretion.
  • Administrative changes must be recorded in an immutable audit log; original point events are not deleted or silently rewritten.

17. Data and leaderboard privacy

  • AquaVaults stores the enrolled wallet, enrollment evidence, verified activity, point events, risk and administrative actions, and distribution records needed to operate and audit the program.
  • The public leaderboard displays a truncated wallet address, but blockchain activity is public and truncation does not guarantee anonymity.
  • Rewards-account records are not reset or migrated to another wallet. Records may be retained for security, fraud prevention, accounting, audit, and legal compliance, subject to applicable law.
  • AquaVaults should collect only information needed for the stated program purposes and protect retained information using controls appropriate to the data.

18. Taxes, legal restrictions, and reporting

  • Participants are solely responsible for determining and satisfying their own tax, reporting, licensing, and legal obligations.
  • AquaVaults may collect, retain, report, withhold, block, or request information where required by applicable law.
  • Participation and distribution are unavailable where prohibited. A wallet may be excluded or a distribution withheld when AquaVaults reasonably determines that proceeding is legally prohibited.
  • Nothing in these rules is tax, legal, accounting, or investment advice.

19. Non-custodial activity and third-party risks

  • AquaVaults does not take custody of participant swap funds through the rewards enrollment process.
  • Participants approve and sign their own blockchain transactions and remain responsible for reviewing transaction details.
  • Swaps, limit orders, LP activity, token prices, liquidity, slippage, network fees, protocol availability, and third-party services involve risk and may fail or change.
  • Points and rewards do not guarantee token performance, profits, reimbursement, or recovery of trading costs.

20. Program changes, suspension, and termination

  • AquaVaults may change point values, tasks, funding allocations, the eligibility percentage, distribution mechanics, or other rules for future cycles by publishing the change before the affected cycle begins.
  • AquaVaults may suspend or terminate the program where necessary for security, legal, treasury, protocol, or operational reasons.
  • A mid-cycle suspension or system-wide task failure may result in a published waiver, adjusted maximum, delayed finalization, or cancellation when completion is not reasonably possible.
  • No rule change creates a guaranteed right to future cycles or future reward pools.
