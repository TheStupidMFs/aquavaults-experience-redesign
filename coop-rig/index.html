Participation builds reward weight during each active reward cycle. Beginning September 1, 2026, reward cycles follow UTC calendar months. Claims may or may not be received during any given cycle. If no claims arrive, nothing is distributed and the next cycle begins fresh.

What is the Co-op Mining Rig?

The Co-op Mining Rig is AquaVaults' participation layer around an AquaVaults-operated rig in the Pond0x ecosystem. AquaVaults manages the interface, Droplet participation, swap queue, accounting, and community activity. Pond0x remains separate and determines which rig swaps count and whether mining claims occur.

Start with your daily Droplets

Verify your Solana wallet, choose the community you want to support, and use up to 6 Droplets per UTC day. Mine, Burn, and Hashboost all draw from the same daily Droplet budget. Droplets are participation credits inside the Co-op Rig; they are not transferable assets.

Mine: choose a route and watch what counts

Each Mine Droplet queues one rig-funded swap attempt on the route you select. After execution, AquaVaults checks Pond0x manifest reporting to see which attempts were counted. An executed swap is not automatically a counted swap, so Swap Route Activity is part of the planning: compare recent results and decide where to apply your next Mine Droplets.

Burn and Hashboost

Burn adds +2 minutes to the next mining session for each Droplet used. Hashboost fills a shared 6-Droplet bucket; a filled bucket queues a 0.01 SOL boost for the next eligible session. Hashboost is limited to 2 Droplets per user per bucket and 3 per user per day.

How sessions start

18 manifest-counted swaps is the base requirement. During boost recovery, the operating target is 21.

Under normal operation, 18 manifest-counted swaps are required to replace the activity needed for a mining session. While the rig is rebuilding its Pond0x mining boost capacity, AquaVaults raises the operating target to 21 manifest-counted swaps so each session requires 3 additional counted swaps above that base.

Those additional counted swaps create surplus activity intended to help the rig climb back toward the full boost capacity Pond0x allows. Once that capacity is full, the target can return to 18. If boost capacity begins to decline again, the target can be raised back to 21.

Mine Droplets create swap attempts; Pond0x decides which attempts count. Only manifest-counted swaps advance the live target shown in Rig Status. This recovery target is separate from the Co-Op Hashboost bucket.

Participation weight

Accepted participation builds reward weight whether or not a Mine attempt later counts in the Pond0x manifest: Mine earns 1 point per Droplet, Burn earns 0.5, and Hashboost earns 1.5. Your selected community receives matching participation credit for the cycle.

Optional Onchain Contribution Mode

Default participation does not require you to fund the rig's swaps. If you enable Onchain Contribution Mode, your contribution also creates a separate small wallet-approved SOL transaction that records participation onchain. This is optional and does not guarantee rewards, claims, swap counting, or recognition by any external platform.

Why the Co-Op exists

For AquaVaults Users

The Co-op Mining Rig gives users a reason to return, participate, build reward weight, and support listed communities.

For Communities

Every selected action contributes equal points to a community. Strong participation can push a community to the top spot for the cycle.

For Pond0x Exposure

AquaVaults users are introduced to the Pond0x ecosystem, mining sessions, claims, and the broader rig model through participation.
