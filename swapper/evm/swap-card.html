💰 Platform Fee (in %) ℹ
Fee for using AquaVaults (separate from network/DEX fees). Split with the selected community based on its active Growth Program share (Standard floor 50/50, Founding floor 60/40, Silver 65/35, Gold 75/25, plus +10% to the community with Partner), or 100% to AquaVaults when AquaVaults is selected. Always charged in the sell token. Enforced server-side.
📊 Slippage (in %) ℹ
Maximum price movement tolerance during swap. Higher slippage allows faster execution but may result in less favorable rates.
⚡ Gas Speed ℹ
Best-effort priority fee tuning. Auto uses your wallet's default. Fast/Turbo raise the priority fee to help include in busy blocks. Overrides are best-effort — your wallet may ignore them.
