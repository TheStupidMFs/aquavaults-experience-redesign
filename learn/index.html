The Core Idea

Communities already have what matters most: attention, users, and coordination. The problem is that most of that activity leaks out to generic swap platforms and creates value somewhere else.

AquaVaults closes that loop. When a community is listed, its members can select that community before they swap. Their activity — trades they were going to make anyway — becomes community-directed revenue.

Same users. Same swaps. Better direction.

How It Works

1

Connect Your Wallet

Use any supported Solana wallet. AquaVaults is non-custodial — you always stay in control of your keys and funds.

2

Select a Community

Choose a listed community you want to support. That selection stays active across swaps and platform activity.

3

Use AquaVaults Tools

Swap, run AutoSwap sessions, set limit orders, or participate in the Co-op Mining Rig — all while your community selection stays active.

4

Activity Generates Value

When a community other than AquaVaults is selected and the platform fee is above 0%, a share of the platform fee routes to that community based on its active Growth Program share, with the remainder routing to AquaVaults. Regular trades become community-directed support.

Why Communities Care

Communities already have people who trade. The question is whether those trades point back to the community or disappear into a generic platform.

AquaVaults gives communities a revenue layer around normal swap behavior — no custom development, no infrastructure, no maintenance required. The call to action is simple: select us before you swap.

Tiered Fee Routing

When a user selects a listed community and swaps, its share of the platform fee follows its Growth Program floor and tier: Standard communities start at 50%, Founding communities have a permanent 60% floor, and qualifying communities may earn 65% Silver or 75% Gold. Partner and reward benefits may apply separately. No pools, no deposits.

Non-Custodial by Design

Users Stay in Control

AquaVaults never holds assets, stores private keys, or executes transactions on your behalf. Every swap is user-signed and confirmed on-chain directly from your wallet.

User-Signed Transactions

Every action requires your approval in your own wallet. AquaVaults provides the interface. You control execution.

Jupiter-Powered Routing

Swap routing and quotes come through Jupiter's infrastructure — one of Solana's most trusted aggregators.

No Deposits Required

Swaps don't require depositing funds into AquaVaults. No AquaVaults smart contracts hold user assets.
