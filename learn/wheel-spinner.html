Free Wheel Spinner

Open AquaVaults Wheel Spinner, start a new wheel and enter up to 25 names, one name per line. Each line is a separate entry, including repeated names.

  1. Load the participant names and check the entry count.
  2. Use a practice spin to review the experience. Practice outcomes are not official results.
  3. Start the official spin. The first official spin locks the entry list.
  4. Run additional spins where eligible entries remain, manage previous-winner exclusions and finish when done.

Free is a temporary browser-local experience, not a cloud-saved event library. Reloading or starting a new Free wheel can replace the temporary drawing. Save any results you need independently before leaving.

Paid Wheel Spinner

Choose a paid capacity for a specific Drawing ID; connect a Solana wallet and approve a one-time payment in supported Solana USDC or USDT. Purchasing a Paid event unlocks organizer features for that drawing only. It does not buy participant entries or prizes, and a second purchase cannot stack additional capacity onto the same event.

Find and Verify Your Paying Wallet

Saved Paid-event cards automatically look up the original paying wallet from the confirmed purchase ledger. Each card can show a shortened paying-wallet address, the currently connected wallet and a control to copy the complete address. This identification alone does not unlock the drawing.

  1. Select the saved Paid event and copy its original paying-wallet address if needed.
  2. In your Solana wallet application, select the account matching that address.
  3. Connect through the AquaVaults header and use Verify original paying wallet under Paid access & recovery.
  4. Approve the ownership-message signature. Only successful server verification unlocks Paid actions and downloads.

If a payment is still processing, retry verification of the original payment instead of paying twice. Keep the Drawing ID and Solana payment signature for support and recovery. An address lookup is available only for an existing confirmed purchase associated with the Drawing ID.

What Paid Recovery Does — and Does Not — Restore

A confirmed purchase is recorded server-side, but the Wheel Spinner's participant names, custom settings and result presentation data remain in the browser that created the event. Reconnecting the original paying wallet can re-establish access to the purchase; it cannot reconstruct names or completed results lost when browser storage is cleared or a device is changed.

Keep the original browser data, retain important exports and do not clear site storage before finishing an event. A successfully verified paid draft may be reopened with its original Drawing ID when the underlying local drawing is unavailable, but missing participant lists are not automatically recovered.

Payment identity is public metadata

The paying-wallet address associated with a confirmed Drawing ID can be looked up by someone who knows that ID. An address is not an authorization credential: no one receives Paid access without demonstrating control of the original wallet.

A Separate Wheel for Listed Communities

AquaVaults also offers a Community Wheel for eligible listed communities. It is part of the community's own Command Center, not the public Free/Paid Wheel Spinner. Community owners can use server-backed drawings, weighted participant imports, community branding, official winner reviews and included exports without purchasing a Public Paid event.

Community Wheel is separately authorized for eligible listed communities and is not accessed through the public Wheel Spinner. Learn about listed Community Wheel capabilities →

Rules, Prizes and Responsible Use

Before running any giveaway, publish clear eligibility and entry rules, deadlines, the prize description, winner-response procedures and the distribution plan. Obtain appropriate consent before uploading participant or wallet information. Comply with laws and platform policies applicable to the location and structure of the promotion; do not use the Wheel to run an unlawful lottery or misleading promotion.

AquaVaults does not independently vet participants, confirm external eligibility, provide prizes or deliver winnings for organizer-operated events. The Wheel records selections and decisions; the organizer handles real-world verification, disputes and fulfillment.
