What Holder Intelligence Does

Holder Intelligence is a research and eligibility tool inside the AquaVaults Community Command Center. It works with Solana NFT collections and token mints. You choose what to check, AquaVaults evaluates the available provider and blockchain evidence, and a successful scan produces a saved holder report.

Use it to identify owners of specific NFTs, find holders who meet token balance or value rules, review exclusions, download the findings, or create a new Community Wheel drawing with those verified entries. Running an audit does not automatically create a giveaway or select a winner.

What “verified” means

A result is based on the evidence the supported data providers and Solana network make available. Current ownership and historical holding are different claims. An acquisition date does not prove a marketplace purchase or the real-world identity of a wallet owner.

NFT Holder Audit

Register a Solana NFT collection with your community, select the collection and run a scan. A normal current-owner scan reports the NFTs the provider identifies in that collection, their current owners, and the eligible wallets after your filters are applied. Burned assets are accounted for separately.

Available NFT filters

  • NFT numbers: select individual numbers or a number range.
  • Traits: match any selected value or require all selected values on the same NFT.
  • Holder count: require a minimum or maximum number of NFTs per wallet; count matching NFTs or all NFTs in that collection.
  • Wallet exclusions: exclude known treasury, team, or other addresses from eligibility.
  • Historical filters: acquired-from, acquired-through, or minimum continuous holding days, when supported history can be independently reconstructed.

Historical NFT verification is an optional, more limited process. It checks acquisition and continuous ownership evidence, rather than simply looking at the current wallet. If evidence is incomplete or the NFT standard is unsupported, a historical scan can be rejected instead of returning an unsupported qualification.

Token Holder Audit

Enter a Solana token mint and choose one of two modes:

1

Current Holder Snapshot

Checks current token account balances, eligible wallet ownership and the token's estimated USD value. This mode does not prove how long tokens have been held.

2

Verified Holding Audit

Also attempts to reconstruct each candidate wallet's uninterrupted current holding period from finalized on-chain transactions. This may take substantially longer or fail if the provider cannot supply complete history.

Available token filters

  • Minimum and optional maximum estimated USD token value.
  • Optional included and excluded wallet lists.
  • Custom include/exclude rules based on wallet, token balance, and USD value, combined with ALL or ANY logic.
  • In Verified mode only, minimum continuous holding hours, holding start date, and historical rules.

Important: token USD values use a third-party Jupiter quote. Prices can be delayed or unavailable, and V1 does not provide an independently certified maximum quote age. Do not treat displayed dollar values as a guarantee of an executable market price.

Saved Reports and Downloads

Successful audits are stored for the authorized community owner. Review the qualifying wallets and available details, see why records were excluded, reopen a saved report, and download the supported CSV or JSON exports. Larger result sets can span multiple pages; the export checks are intended to reconcile the downloaded total with the saved report.

Optional Community Wheel Integration

Choose a successfully saved scan to create a new Community Wheel draft based on its eligible entries. The original audit is not changed.

  • NFT: one chance per eligible wallet, or one chance per qualifying NFT. Per-NFT tickets can display the wallet, NFT number, or both while preserving the underlying mint-to-owner mapping.
  • Token: equal chances, value-based tiers, holding-duration tiers, or a combined model where the saved report supports those fields. Configurable weights are capped at nine chances per wallet.
  • Capacity: Community Wheel has its own limits, including up to 10,000 distinct entry records and up to 50,000 weighted tickets for supported configurations. Not every audit will fit every drawing mode.

The community organizer remains responsible for giveaway rules, consent, eligibility, prizes, awarding and legal compliance. See the Community Wheel guide and Terms of Service.

What Holder Intelligence Cannot Prove

  • That an NFT holder originally minted or purchased an NFT, rather than receiving a transfer.
  • Who controls a wallet in the real world, or whether multiple wallets belong to one person.
  • That the collection or token is legitimate, safe, appropriately priced or eligible for a particular promotion.
  • That all historical formats or unlimited holder populations can be verified in one audit.
  • That a token's displayed USD price is independently time-certified or executable at that value.
  • That a scan substitutes for an organizer's own giveaway rules, participant verification, legal duties or prize delivery.

Getting Started

If you're an authorized listed-community owner, open your Community Command Center and select Holder Intelligence. Choose NFT Audit or Token Audit, enter the source and filters, review the scope and limits, then submit your scan. Token jobs may continue processing after you leave the page; use the job list to check their status.

When the audit completes, review the exclusions and totals before downloading exports or creating an optional Wheel drawing. To learn about listing a community, see Get Listed.
