Your Account

One wallet. One Account.

AquaVaults Account brings eligible participation and optional Connector enrollment into one wallet-bound view. Your wallet stays in your control.

What an Account Does

Account helps you access a common overview of User Rewards, Co-Op Mining Rig participation, and optional Connector activity. It is an identity and reporting layer—not a deposit account, exchange account, custody service, or wallet transfer permission.

Connect Wallet connects a Solana wallet to supported AquaVaults tools. Sign In verifies control of that wallet with a signed message and creates an authenticated Account session. Connecting alone does not sign you in or authorize trading.

Create an Account or Sign In

  1. Open AquaVaults Account and connect your supported Solana wallet.
  2. If you already have an Account, select Sign In and approve the wallet-control message.
  3. If this is your first Account, select Review Account Creation. Read the presented AquaVaults Terms of Service, User Rewards rules, and Co-Op acknowledgment, then approve the separate Account creation message.
  4. After authentication, use the Overview, Rewards, Co-Op, and Connector tabs to review available program information.

These are signed messages, not transactions. Account sign-in does not transfer tokens, start mining, queue swaps, or guarantee any rewards.

Connector

Optionally register a permanent referral code and reward wallet, view applications and verified linked communities, and review Floor Upgrade indicators. Learn the Connector rules.

Wallet and Session Safety

Private Account information requires an authenticated session for the same connected Solana wallet. If you disconnect or switch wallets, Account-specific information must not be shown for a different wallet. Sign out of the existing Account before using a different identity; sign in again when the session expires.

Wallet ownership and private keys remain with you. Never share your recovery phrase or private key, and do not interpret a message-signing request as an invitation to approve a token transfer.

Connector registration is additional, optional consent. Its public code and recipient wallet cannot be rotated after registration. A separately signed permanent payout halt is irreversible; read its warning carefully before using it.

What Account Does Not Promise

Creating an Account does not grant an existing community listing, guarantee monthly User Rewards, create a Connector reward, or change community fee routing. Community Floor Upgrades are owner-controlled purchases governed by their own eligibility window and verification rules. Review the applicable Terms of Service and individual program rules.
