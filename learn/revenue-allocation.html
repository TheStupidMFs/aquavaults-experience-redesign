Revenue Allocation & Official Wallets

Learn how AquaVaults plans to allocate verified platform revenue and review the official Solana wallets used for revenue custody, allocation recipients, operations, payouts, subscriptions, and program participation.

Important

Not every wallet listed on this page receives a percentage of platform revenue. Each wallet is labeled according to its specific role so its purpose can be independently reviewed on-chain. A wallet balance is not automatically allocatable revenue.

How AquaVaults Revenue Is Allocated

Verified allocatable AquaVaults revenue is assigned according to the following plan.

50% User Growth Participation rewards, adoption campaigns, and ecosystem growth.
30% Operations Infrastructure, development, and platform maintenance.
10% Strategic Reserve Long-term flexibility, stability, and future strategic needs.
5% Community Support Approved community initiatives and listed-community participation.
5% Co-Op The AquaVaults Co-Op ecosystem and related participation systems.
Note

Allocation percentages apply only to revenue that has been verified and classified as allocatable. Opening balances, protected reserves, externally controlled funds, purpose-restricted holdings, and unrelated wallet assets are excluded. When an amount cannot be divided perfectly at the asset's smallest raw-unit level, the indivisible remainder is assigned to Operations.

Protected Operational Reserves

Several AquaVaults wallets maintain native SOL for transaction execution, account creation, and uninterrupted platform operation. These balances are operating reserves, not separate allocation categories or distributable revenue.

Revenue Treasury 0.25 SOL

The Revenue Treasury reserve is funded through Operations and protected from normal allocation. Vault Creation and DFS Creation are also maintained using Operations-controlled funds. A shortfall in either operational wallet does not create a new allocation category or reduce another recipient's published percentage.

Understanding On-Chain Wallet Balances

Solana explorers provide an independent view of each wallet's current on-chain holdings. A visible balance should not be interpreted as fully allocatable AquaVaults revenue. A wallet may contain:

  • Opening or historical balances
  • Protected native-SOL reserves
  • Revenue awaiting verification
  • Small balances carried forward into a future period
  • Assets that cannot yet be safely converted
  • Program participation assets
  • Funds reserved for a specific payment or operating purpose
  • Previously allocated funds awaiting their intended use

AquaVaults applies the published allocation percentages only to revenue that has been verified and classified as allocatable.

Wallet Classifications

Every address on this page is classified by one or more of the six roles below. Some wallets serve more than one role and display multiple badges accordingly.

Revenue Custody

Holds verified revenue before approved processing, allocation, or distribution.

Allocation Recipient

Receives a defined percentage under the published revenue allocation plan.

Operational

Supports a specific platform execution, maintenance, or account-creation function.

Payout Wallet

Holds funds designated for approved program, connector, or partner payments.

Revenue Intake

Receives payment for a defined AquaVaults product or service before revenue verification and accounting.

Program Participation

Holds an asset or position used to access an external network, testing environment, or strategic program.
