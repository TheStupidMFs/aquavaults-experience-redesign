For Eligible Listed Communities

Community Wheel is separate from the public Wheel Spinner. It is available only to eligible listed communities through private, authorized access. Community owners can manage drawings, while members without management permission have view-only access.

Separate from Public Paid

Community Wheel uses the listed community's dashboard authorization. It does not require purchasing a public Paid drawing or signing a paying-wallet challenge to manage an eligible community drawing.

Built for Listed Community Giveaways

Use the Community Wheel to run documented giveaways for your community with reusable branding, structured participant imports, official server-selected outcomes and downloadable result records. It is included with eligible listed-community access; active Partner status adds retention and reusable-library benefits.

Participant Imports, Tickets and Wallet Rules

Owners can enter plain names, import CSV, TXT or XLSX files, or create a new draft from an eligible server-verified Holder Intelligence scan. An advanced participant record may carry a unique entry ID, display name, optional validated wallet address and integer ticket weight. Duplicate names remain distinct entries unless an explicit import rule changes that behavior.

Each drawing can contain up to 10,000 distinct participant records and 50,000 weighted tickets. Ticket weights affect selection odds. The dashboard provides a preview and validation before the participant list is saved.

Winner eligibility modes include each entry can win once, allow repeat winners, or one accepted win per wallet. Wallet grouping requires actual wallet fields stored with participant records; a wallet-looking display name is not an automatic mapping.

Practice, Official Spins and Winner Decisions

Configure a fixed number of prizes or an open-ended drawing that the owner explicitly ends, within the supported 100-round limit. Practice spins are visual-only and never add a candidate to the official record.

Starting the official drawing locks its roster and governing rules. The backend selects an official candidate before the wheel animation. The owner can accept the selection, reject it with a supported reason, or record an unawarded/skip outcome where the drawing mode allows. Decisions are recorded with the official event history rather than rerolling silently.

During an interrupted session, reopen the same community drawing and synchronize with the recorded server state. The browser animation is presentation, not an alternate source of winner selection.

Community Branding, Reports and Replays

Choose the standard AquaVaults look, verified listed-community logo and colors, or advanced appearance settings. Advanced design options include backgrounds, palettes, logo sizing and event text. Preview the presentation before saving the final design.

Official results can be downloaded in supported CSV, JSON, PNG and PDF forms, along with winner-focused reports, celebration packs and reconstructed spin-replay videos. Replays present saved selections; they are not recordings of the original spin and never select new winners. Owner audit exports can contain private mapping details and should not be distributed without review.

Founding communities: Owners receive the full standard Community Wheel drawing tools, plus included Holder Intelligence scans that can generate eligible verified NFT or token-holder participant data for a new Wheel draft. Founding status alone does not grant the active Partner template library or 180-day Wheel drawing retention. If a Founding community also holds an active Partner subscription, those Partner benefits apply to newly created drawings.

Community Wheel drawing retention and Holder Intelligence scan/report retention are separate. Active Partner owners can save up to 25 reusable appearance, participant or complete-setup templates. Retention is set when each drawing is created, so a later upgrade does not automatically extend an existing Wheel. Export records before expiration.

Optional Permanent Solana Result Publication

Before official start, an owner may choose Publish final results on Solana. If enabled, accepted winner names and unawarded prize outcomes from a completed drawing may be published as readable Solana memos. These names can become publicly searchable and effectively permanent. Publication cannot be switched on or off after the drawing starts.

Only enable this if your event notices explain that names may be published permanently and the necessary permissions have been obtained. Community publication is independent of the on-chain transaction used to purchase public Paid Wheel Spinner software.

Rules, Prizes and Responsible Use

Before running any giveaway, publish clear eligibility and entry rules, deadlines, the prize description, winner-response procedures and the distribution plan. Obtain appropriate consent before uploading participant or wallet information. Comply with laws and platform policies applicable to the location and structure of the promotion; do not use the Wheel to run an unlawful lottery or misleading promotion.

AquaVaults does not independently vet participants, confirm external eligibility, provide prizes or deliver winnings for organizer-operated events. The Wheel records selections and decisions; the organizer handles real-world verification, disputes and fulfillment.
