For Communities

Community Campaigns

Time-boxed community competitions with frozen rules, server-authoritative scoring, transparent standings, and temporary Growth Program rewards for qualifying placements.

What Is a Community Campaign?

AquaVaults Community Campaigns are competitions between listed communities. Campaign points belong to communities through AquaVaults' existing attribution systems; they are not individual-wallet competitions.

Every Campaign has its own scoring window and an immutable template that defines the metric, point conversion, competition structure, and reward rules. Only qualifying activity inside that Campaign's scoring window earns Campaign points.

Separate From User Rewards

Community Campaigns and AquaVaults User Rewards are different programs. A community's Campaign score does not automatically create User Rewards points, and User Rewards participation does not automatically create Campaign eligibility.

Current Campaign Templates

A Campaign template is the immutable scoring contract for a Campaign. It defines the authoritative activity source, what metric is measured, how that metric converts into points, whether historical placement mechanics apply, and what reward ladder can be used.

Template versions are immutable. If AquaVaults changes a template's competitive meaning, the change is published as a new version rather than silently rewriting an already-announced Campaign.

Community Fee Race

Measures qualifying AquaVaults platform fees generated for community-attributed activity.

Points: $0.01 of qualifying platform fee = 1 Campaign point.

🌊

Community Volume Race

Measures qualifying Solana trading volume attributed to each competing community.

Points: 0.01 SOL of qualifying volume = 1 Campaign point.

🔁

Community Swap Race

Counts qualifying swaps rather than weighting communities by the size of each trade.

Points: 1 qualifying swap = 100 Campaign points. A counted swap must generate at least $0.05 in platform fee under the current template.

⚙️

AutoSwap Challenge

Measures qualifying fee activity produced through AquaVaults AutoSwap rather than general manual trading.

Points: $0.01 of qualifying AutoSwap fee = 1 Campaign point.

↔️

Flip-Flop Challenge

Measures qualifying fee activity attributed specifically to Flip-Flop behavior.

Points: $0.01 of qualifying Flip-Flop fee = 1 Campaign point.

🔗

Widget Adoption Race

Measures qualifying activity from approved AquaVaults Widget origins. Campaign credit follows the approved origin-owner community, while normal fee routing still follows the community selected for the swap.

Points: $0.01 of qualifying Widget fee = 1 Campaign point.

🎯

Sponsored Token Buy Race

A target token and sponsor community are frozen for the Campaign. Direct target-token buys receive full weight; direct target-token sells receive one-quarter weight. Merely passing through the token as an intermediate route does not qualify.

Points: 0.01 SOL of adjusted target-token volume = 1 Campaign point.

⛏️

Co-Op Mining Race

Measures authoritative Co-Op points credited to each competing community.

Points: 0.5 authoritative Co-Op point = 50 Campaign points.

🤝

Co-Op Participation Race

Counts qualifying unique Co-Op contributors credited to each competing community.

Points: 1 qualifying unique Co-Op contributor = 100 Campaign points.

Public-Ready Formats

The formats above are the Campaign templates AquaVaults currently presents as ready for public Campaign launches. Additional template definitions can remain in the engine for historical or validation purposes without being advertised as available for a new public Campaign.

Placement Depends on the Template

Current trading, automation, Widget, and Sponsored Token formats use scale-sensitive placement balancing: historical activity can determine divisions and a 1.00×–1.50× handicap at T-7, but history never contributes points to the active Campaign. Current Co-Op formats instead use one frozen competition field with no historical-strength divisions or placement handicap. The exact frozen placement is shown separately from live scoring.

How a Campaign Moves From Announcement to Final Results

1

Campaign Rules Are Announced

AquaVaults identifies the Campaign template, scoring dates, eligibility class, and reward mode. Competitive rules are locked at announcement.

2

The Eligible Roster Freezes Before Scoring

At the Campaign's T-7 placement cutoff, eligible communities are frozen. When the template uses divisions or handicaps, those values freeze at the same time.

3

The Scoring Window Opens

Only qualifying activity inside the published scoring window counts. Public standings, when enabled for that Campaign, refresh from server-owned Campaign data.

4

AquaVaults Reconciles and Finalizes

After scoring ends, AquaVaults rebuilds the full authoritative window, reviews required integrity issues, and explicitly finalizes the official result.

5

Earned Rewards Are Scheduled Separately

Finalization determines what was earned; it does not itself change fee routing. An earned Campaign reward is scheduled separately for its defined full UTC reward month.

Who Is Eligible?

The exact eligibility class is part of the Campaign rules. Current Campaign templates can be configured for all listed communities, active Partner communities, or Founding communities.

Eligibility is evaluated at the T-7 cutoff. A community added after announcement but before T-7 can still participate when it satisfies the announced class. After T-7, the frozen roster does not rebalance because a community later withdraws or is disqualified.

AquaVaults itself is never a Campaign participant.

Historical Strength, Divisions, and Handicaps

Historical activity is used only to make certain Campaigns more competitively balanced. It never gives a community points in the current Campaign.

For templates that use scale-sensitive placement, AquaVaults looks at full completed UTC calendar months of the relevant historical metric. The structural lookback is six completed months and the handicap lookback is three completed months.

Depending on the frozen eligible roster and the template, a Campaign may run as one field or multiple divisions. Current divided templates use a gap-based handicap from 1.00× to a maximum of 1.50×, with stronger historical communities receiving less adjustment and weaker historical communities receiving more.

New Communities

A community without a full historical month is not forced into made-up history. The Campaign engine has a newcomer path, including a server-calculated safeguard that can require operator review before placement freezes.

Points and Ranking

Each Campaign template defines how its authoritative metric converts into Campaign points. Examples can include platform fees generated, qualifying SOL-equivalent volume, counted swaps, qualifying automated activity, widget activity, or another template-specific source.

Displayed Campaign points are whole numbers, but the ranking engine preserves the exact pre-floor score. That means two communities can display the same whole-number point total while still being ordered correctly by the underlying exact calculation.

When two communities have a true exact tie, the Campaign's published tie rules determine the occupied placements and any pooled reward treatment.

Public Standings and the Eligible Roster

When public standings are enabled for a Campaign, the public leaderboard shows only active communities that currently have a positive score. A legitimate zero remains part of the Campaign record but is not displayed as a public leaderboard result.

The full frozen eligible roster is shown separately from positive-score standings. That roster records who was eligible when placement froze; a later withdrawal or integrity decision does not rewrite the original eligibility record.

Live Means Provisional

Live points and ranks can change as activity is processed, participants continue scoring, integrity decisions are applied, and the final scoring window is reconciled. Only finalized results are official.

Withdrawals and Integrity

An eligible community may withdraw before scoring ends under the applicable Campaign rules. Withdrawal removes it from public standings and reward eligibility, but AquaVaults retains its Campaign activity and continues private shadow scoring for audit and possible approved reinstatement.

Deliberate community-level manipulation can result in disqualification for the Campaign. AquaVaults retains the underlying activity and evidence rather than rewriting history. Objective source failures can block finalization until resolved; suspicious but non-conclusive anomalies require review rather than an arbitrary manual score edit.

Campaign Rewards

Campaign rewards are temporary Growth Program fee-share effects earned from final Campaign placement. They do not permanently rewrite a community's Growth Program tier or guaranteed floor.

Under the current Standard reward model, a single-field Campaign awards +10 percentage points to first place and +5 percentage points to second through fourth. When a Campaign uses two or more divisions, each standard division awards +10 percentage points to first and +5 percentage points to second.

The exact reward rules announced for a Campaign control that Campaign. Reward application is separately validated so a community's effective fee share cannot exceed the applicable program maximum.

Not Every Internal Campaign Is Public

AquaVaults may operate internal, private, test, or validation Campaigns without publishing their public standings. The public Campaign page only displays a Campaign when AquaVaults has enabled its public standings and the applicable public display conditions are met.

A public Learn article, Campaign page, template capability, or dashboard field does not guarantee that a particular Campaign is open, that a community is eligible, or that any reward will be earned.

Campaign-Key Analytics Are Different

Some AquaVaults analytics use a campaign or campaign-key attribution field to record where a deep-link interaction came from. That is source tracking only. It is not the Community Campaign system and does not create Campaign points, eligibility, placement, or rewards.
