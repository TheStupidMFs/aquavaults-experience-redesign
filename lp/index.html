How AquaVaults LP fee routing works. AquaVaults never takes custody of your LP position. When a supported position has claimable trading fees, you choose a community and how much of those claimable fees to keep or share. Saving those support settings signs a message only; claiming fees requires a separate wallet-approved transaction. Pools can pay claimable fees in both tokens, Token B only, or use Meteora Compounding. AquaVaults shows the actual token ticker and amount for each claimable fee before you sign.
