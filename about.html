About AquaVaults

A Solana-native interface for swaps, automation, and community-driven activity — with user-signed onchain execution where supported and optional wallet-linked participation features.

1,010+ Mining Sessions
Solana Native On-Chain Execution
Non-Custodial Your Keys, Your Assets

AquaVaults is a non-custodial interface built to make on-chain activity on Solana feel more direct, configurable, and useful. You connect your own wallet, review the transaction, and sign directly. AquaVaults does not hold assets, store private keys, or execute transactions on your behalf.

Whether you're making a quick swap, setting up structured activity, or choosing a community to support through platform fees, the goal is the same: give you more control over how you move on-chain.

Swap routing and price quotes are provided through Jupiter's routing infrastructure, which aggregates available liquidity sources across Solana. Transactions execute on-chain through third-party protocols.

⛏ Co-op Mining Rig

Participate Beyond Swaps

AquaVaults includes a participation layer called the Co-op Mining Rig, designed to give users a way to engage with the platform beyond swaps.

The system connects to an AquaVaults-owned mining rig operating within the Pond0x ecosystem. AquaVaults provides the interface and participation tracking, while Pond0x powers the underlying mining activity.

Participation is tracked using Droplets, which are distributed daily and used to contribute toward mining sessions, extend runtime, or support shared system mechanics. Default Droplet participation does not require trading or providing funds to the mining rig.

Users may also enable optional Onchain Contribution Mode, which adds a small wallet-approved SOL transaction to each Co-op contribution so participation can be recorded onchain. This is optional and does not guarantee rewards, claims, or outcomes.

The Co-op Mining Rig is designed to reward participation, support listed communities, and introduce users to the broader Pond0x ecosystem through a structured and accessible interface.

Built for Communities Too

AquaVaults is not just a swap interface for individuals. It is also designed to give communities a way to stay active on-chain and benefit from that activity.

When a user selects a community and keeps the platform fee enabled, a portion of that platform fee can route directly to the community's designated on-chain account within the same signed transaction. That creates a simple on-chain way for users to support the groups they are part of while still using the tools they already need.

For communities, that can mean new support for rewards, visibility, growth initiatives, or ecosystem activity. For users, it creates a more meaningful way to participate while swapping.

AquaVaults does not custody, pool, or distribute community funds after settlement. Selection does not constitute endorsement, partnership, or sponsorship.
