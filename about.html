Why it exists

The people driving Web3 deserve more than recognition.

Communities create attention, education, conversation, and real onchain activity. Traditional swap platforms benefit from that activity, while the groups responsible for it often receive nothing.

AquaVaults changes the direction of that value. Instead of treating community as a marketing layer around the transaction, the community becomes part of the transaction's purpose.

Control stays with you

Your wallet. Your signature. Your choice.

Non-custodial

AquaVaults does not hold your assets or store your private keys.

Review before signing

You inspect transaction details and approve activity directly in your wallet.

Transparent routing

Supported fee routing is included within the transaction and can be reviewed onchain.

Built for both sides

Useful tools for users. Sustainable infrastructure for communities.

Users get access to swaps, automation, limit orders, multi-chain tools, rewards, liquidity systems, and community discovery without giving up wallet control.

Listed communities gain a practical way to turn the activity they already inspire into revenue, visibility, campaigns, and longer-term onchain participation.
