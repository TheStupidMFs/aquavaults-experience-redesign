How it works

Permanent referral records. No automatic commissions.

ENROLL

Create your Connector identity

When enrollment is available, AquaVaults Account holders can review and sign separate Connector consent. Registration creates a permanent public code and binds the current Account wallet as the immutable reward wallet.

INTRODUCE

Refer a community

When applications are available, communities may submit through your code. A referral code alone is not listing approval, historical attribution, or authority to represent a community.

VERIFY

Verify the introduction

After an independent listing review and production verification, AquaVaults may record the introducing Connector permanently. That private record does not establish an official community affiliation or a cash payment by itself.
